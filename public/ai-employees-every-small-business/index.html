What an AI employee is (and what it is not)

An AI employee has a role, a goal, tools and a memory. It gets a job ("answer every quote request within five minutes"), does it, and reports back. A chatbot waits for questions. An employee does the work.

It is not magic, and it is not free of mistakes. It needs a clear job, good instructions and a human who checks its work, the same as a new hire.

The Klarna warning

The loudest AI story in customer service is also the best warning. In February 2024 Klarna said its AI assistant did "the equivalent work of 700 full-time agents" (Klarna). In May 2025 its CEO said cost "seems to have been a too predominant evaluation factor" and "what you end up having is lower quality" (CX Today). Klarna kept the AI and brought humans back.

My lesson: AI employees win when you judge them on the quality of the work, not only on what they save. Give them the repeat work. Keep people for judgment, trust and the hard cases. I wrote more on this on the Dooza blog.
